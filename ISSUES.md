# Current Issues

- Networking scripting currently uses one shared Steam lobby session per process. It has messages, RPCs and host-written variables, but no automatic network spawn/ownership, prediction/rollback, dedicated-server transport or host migration. A lobby owner change ends the scripting session; explicitly host/join again. Real Steam relay behavior still needs two-account/two-PC testing; the automated checks use an in-memory transport. See `Docs/markdown/Networking_Scripting.md`.

- NPC targets/patrol points and particle sub-emitter targets still resolve by gameobject name. Their new drop slots preserve this format: duplicate names remain ambiguous, renaming requires dropping the target again, and patrol marker names cannot contain commas. Script gameobject fields use scene IDs and survive renames.

- Turning off CPU culling stops moved objects from updating.
- Material batching is off by default.
The final image is drawn twice per frame.
- Shader hot reload points at an old, hard-coded mgcb.exe path, so in practice it doesn't work.
- `Tests/Components --graphics` stops at the pause menu check "the document is filled in: settings rows, scene line, and the session card" (`PauseChecks.cs`): it expects 9 settings rows in the PersistenceTest pause menu. Not caused by the weather changes; the pause menu/MenuSettings files are unmodified.
- Raindrops on surfaces and the ground mist are not occluded: drops bead on surfaces under a roof and the mist fills rooms below its top (untick the material's Rain Drops for sheltered surfaces).
- Breakables (`Breakable` component) fracture once when Play starts: the pattern doesn't get finer around the hit, pieces don't break again or collide with each other, curved shards' convex hull colliders rest a little proud of the floor, open meshes get no cap on some cuts, and blood splats are floor particles, not decals (`Docs/markdown/Breakables.md` > Limitations).
- Breakable glass shards have no sounds or particles yet, and falling shards don't collide with each other (`Docs/markdown/Breakable_Glass.md` > Limitations). 
- The REA MCP server (`rea-agents@6.7.0 mcp`) times out on connect in Claude Code (30 s), although `doctor` reports its registration as aligned; Ghidra isn't wired for it (`GHIDRA_INSTALL_DIR` unset; Ghidra is at `C:DevToolsghidra_12.1.4_PUBLIC`). (common issue across platforms, not a priority)
- The main view's near plane is hard-coded to 1 m (`Renderer.cs`, `CreatePerspectiveFieldOfView(camera.FieldOfView, aspect, 1, g_farplane)`): anything closer than 1 m to the camera is clipped, e.g. a bottle held up to a first-person camera or a close-up of small props. Lowering it needs checking the depth reconstruction and the passes that assume it.
- Bottle liquid (Liquid component) isn't drawn into shadow maps or Planar Reflection mirror images (like the glass around it), and containers on their side always use the +X side fill table, so non-round containers lying on another side fill a little off (`Docs/markdown/Bottle_Liquid.md` > Limits).
- The image skybox sun (Environment > Skybox > Sun glare) is only drawn on the visible sky: Planar Reflection mirrors (`PlanarSky` in `Forward.fx`) and reflection probe captures show the panorama's plain LDR sun. In scenes with thick volumetric fog in front of the sky (e.g. LookDev) the sun is mostly hidden behind it.
- The AI texture generator's normal map convention is not confirmed in the engine yet (`Docs/Research/AiTextures/PLAN.md` task 0.2): the `NormalConventionTest` captures show almost no sun on the brick slabs (a low directional light barely changes the image next to the sky lighting), so they can't tell DirectX from OpenGL. Poly Haven's DirectX maps already look right in `WeatherTest`, so `texgen` writes DirectX / Y- by default; a person needs to confirm, or the test needs a point light close to the slabs.
- `sdxl_classic` makes textures with many small elements: "red brick wall" gives ~15 courses per tile, so with the family's `scale_m` the bricks come out ~5 cm; the prompt suffixes don't say how close the view is. `sdxl_chord` makes materials in about 45 s on an RTX 3060 and its license is research-only (copyleft), so don't ship content made with it in a commercial game without checking; the open CHORD weights predict no height map (derived from the albedo). The texgen seam check can't tell a tile border from a joint that happens to start there with fewer than ~5 % of columns being strong edges, so it is deliberately lenient (`texgen/seams.py`).
- Importing a generated material (`GeneratedMaterialImporter`, like `AssetImporter`) runs mgcb synchronously on the game thread, so the editor freezes for the length of the content build (seconds, longer when shaders or models are out of date).
- Built-in models `sphere.x`, `dragon_uv_smooth.fbx` and the editor arrows are built without tangent frames: a material with a normal map on them crashes the renderer like `IsoSphere` did (fixed there). Set `GenerateTangentFrames=True` in `Content.mgcb` for any that gets one.
- The texgen `classic` backend is still a little off the Poly Haven references: normals ~1.2-1.4x too strong, stone/dirt roughness ~0.15 too high and not following the reference's structure; a flat input (Metal063) keeps a little invented relief (normal tilt 0.18 against 0.006), and dark metals fall below the `metal` albedo range (>= 140 sRGB) (`Docs/Research/AiTextures/D2_CALIBRATION.md`).
- Generated materials vs the Source 2 look (pipeline version 5, `Docs/Research/AiTextures/D2_CALIBRATION.md` > Source 2 look check, Pipeline version 5): the per-family albedo bands and warmth targets come from real-world photo albedos and a handful of lit, tonemapped HLA screenshot crops, not HLA's actual albedo. SDXL/CHORD images are still cleaner than HLA's surfaces (HLA plaster carries dense mottled grime, its bricks are a muddier red); the style only grades colour and contrast and can't add that. `classic` from a small photo stays soft and repeats visibly, `make_tileable` ghosts strong features near the photo's borders, and thin dark outlines baked into a photo still show faintly. The MaterialLab cube's side faces show textures turned 90 degrees and its warm low sun tints everything orange (use `Scenes/Source2MaterialTest.obsc`).
- `Engine.exe` started from another working directory than its own (e.g. `Engine\bin\Debug\net10.0-windows\Engine.exe` from the repo root, as `Docs/markdown/Generated_Materials.md` shows) crashes in `SdfGenerator.GenerateDistanceFields` writing `Content/GameObjects/Default/cube.sdft` relative to the current directory. Run it from its own folder (absolute `--import-generated`/`--shot` paths).
- The parallax G-buffer pass (`DrawTextureDisplacement` in `Gbuffer.fx`, used for any material with albedo + normal + displacement maps) never samples the roughness or metallic map: Material components get them back through `WriteBuffers`' component override, but a model's own imported material with a height map renders with its scalar roughness/metallic.
